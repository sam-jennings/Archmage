ARCHMAGE ASCENSION

Online Rules · The Full Reference
🎴

Deck Selection

  • 2–4 players: four energies × ranks 1–15 (60 cards + 2 wilds = 62 cards)
  • 5–6 players: five energies incl. Echo × ranks 1–15 (75 cards + 4 wilds = 79 cards)

Echo is the fifth energy — 5–6 player games only. Start with 1 counter (2–4p) or 2 counters (5–6p).

🃏

Deal & Layout

  • All players: 7 cards each
  • The Array: 5 cards face-up
  • The Source: remaining cards face-down
  • First player: determined randomly
⚡

The Opening

Before the first player's turn:

Each player simultaneously chooses 1 component from their hand and places it in the Arcane Reserve (the discard pile)

This guarantees the Released Reserve holds enough for final preparations when the Drought comes

🔮

Starting Counters

Each player takes 1 counter

Counters represent your magical capacity for:

  • Casting spells
  • Taking learning actions
🌟

The Four Energies

Radiance The emissive current — warm and gold.
Void The absorptive current — quiet, deep violet.
Flux The transitive current — cold teal, always moving.
Aether The stabilizing current — crimson, exact, binding.
Wild Substitutes for any energy and value.
Each card is a magical component: an energy + a value (1–15). Example: Void 7 is a Void-attuned component with value 7.
🗝️

Key Reminders

  • Wilds substitute for any energy and value.
  • Enchantments are always active — never cast.
  • Counters are capacity, not a resource — all return at Learning.
🔮

Conjuration

Same Energy

Requirements

3+ components of the same energy type

Benefit When Cast

Power Generation: Draw extra components

  • 3-5 components: +1 component
  • 6+ components: +2 components
🔄

Transfiguration

Sequential Values

Requirements

3+ components in sequence (any energies)

Cannot loop around (14-15-1 invalid)
1-2-3 and 13-14-15 are valid

Benefit When Cast

Transformation Rights: exchange through the Array

  • 3 components: Discard 2 from hand, take 1 from the Array
  • 4+ components: Discard 1 from hand, take 1 from the Array
⚡

Enchantment

Matching Values

Requirements

3-5 components of the same value (5-component needs the Echo suit — 5-6 player games)

Benefit (Always Active)

Enhanced Capability — capacity is measured in counters (each counter = 1 spell cast + 1 learning action per turn):

  • 3 components: +1 counter
  • 4 components: +3 counters
  • 5 components: +5 counters
When learned: gain +1 counter (3-card), +3 counters (4-card), or +5 counters (5-card). Enchantments score 4 / 10 / 16 Recognition Points (3 / 4 / 5-card).
✨

Perfect Transmutation

Sequential Same Energy

Requirements

3+ components in sequence of the same energy type

E.g., Void 8–9–10 (cannot loop)

Benefit When Cast

Ultimate Power: grants BOTH effects, in order — both mandatory

  • Conjuration effect (bonus draws from the Source)
  • Transfiguration effect (exchange through the Array)

Still uses only 1 counter when cast

Turn Sequence

1. Collection

Draw 1 from the Source, or take 1 from the Array (replace from Source)

2. Casting

Cast spells one at a time, placing a counter on each. Resolve effects immediately

↩ Recall

Retrieve all counters from cast spells back into your pool

3. Learning

Use your capacity for Learn / Empower / Reshape / Unlearn (1 counter each)

⚡

Understanding Counters

Casting Phase

Counters represent spells you can cast

Place them on spells to activate their benefits

↩ Recall

Pick up all counters from cast spells — return them to your pool

This is a named step, not just a rule to remember

Learning Phase

Same counters now represent learning actions available

Use them to Learn/Empower/Reshape/Unlearn

📥

1. Collection Phase

Choose one

  • Draw 1 from the Source, OR
  • Take 1 from the Array (immediately replace from the Source)
That's it — no extra draws here. Conjuration bonus draws happen during the Casting Phase when you cast the spell.
⚡

2. Casting Phase

Activate spells by placing 1 counter per spell. Effects resolve immediately — one spell at a time.

Casting Conjuration

  • 3–5 cards: draw +1 from the Source
  • 6+ cards: draw +2 from the Source

Casting Transfiguration

  • 3-card: discard 2 from hand → take 1 from the Array
  • 4+-card: discard 1 from hand → take 1 from the Array

Discarded components go to the Arcane Reserve (not back to the Array). Replace the Array card from the Source immediately.

Casting Perfect Transmutation

  • Resolve both effects in order: Conjuration draws, then the Transfiguration exchange — both mandatory
Can't resolve = can't cast. If you lack the cards to complete a Transfiguration exchange, you cannot cast that spell this turn.
🎲

Counter Management

Gaining Counters

  • Start: 1 counter (2-4 players) / 2 counters (5-6 players)
  • Learn 3-component Enchantment: +1 counter
  • Learn 4-component Enchantment: +3 counters
  • Learn 5-component Enchantment: +5 counters

Losing Counters

  • Break/Unlearn 3-card Enchantment: −1 counter
  • Break/Unlearn 4-card Enchantment: −3 counters
  • Break/Unlearn 5-card Enchantment: −5 counters
  • Step an Enchantment down one size — lose only the ladder difference: 5→4 = −2, 4→3 = −2, 3→dissolved = −1
Timing (F3): capacity you gain from an Enchantment applies from the start of your next turn; capacity you lose (reduce or dissolve) applies immediately. You cannot reduce or dissolve an Enchantment unless you can pay the action (1 counter) and absorb the capacity loss from your currently available counters.
🎯

Strategic Casting

Example with 2 counters:

Option 1: Cast 2 Conjurations → Maximum gathering

Option 2: Cast 1 Conjuration + 1 Transfiguration → Gathering + transforming

Perfect Transmutation uses only 1 counter but grants both benefits!
💫

Enchantments Special Rules

  • Always active - no casting needed
  • Don't use counters for activation
  • Automatically boost your capacity
  • Stack with each other
3 three-component Enchantments = 4 total counters (1 base + 3 bonus)
📚

3. Learning Phase

Recall first: pick up all counters from your cast spells and return them to your pool. Those counters now fund your learning actions.

Actions available

  • Base: 1 action (2 at 5-6 players)  |  3-card Enchantment: +1  |  4-card Enchantment: +3  |  5-card Enchantment: +5

Actions (1 counter each)

  • LEARN: Place a valid spell from hand into your Spellbook
    3-card Enchantment → +1 counter; 4-card → +3 counters; 5-card → +5 counters. Enchantment capacity becomes available from your next turn.
  • EMPOWER: Add components from hand to an existing spell; may convert among Conjuration / Transfiguration / Perfect Transmutation, but NOT into or out of an Enchantment (use LEARN/UNLEARN for that)
  • RESHAPE: Break down one or more spells and rebuild with the same components — all must remain valid; costs one counter per spell broken down (N spells = N counters)
    ⚠ Breaking a 3-card Enchantment: −1 counter; a 4-card: −3 counters; a 5-card: −5 counters. Reducing an Enchantment's size loses capacity by the ladder difference immediately (5→4 −2, 4→3 −2, 3→dissolve −1).
  • UNLEARN: Return one or more components from a single spell to hand; the remainder stays a valid spell or the spell dissolves. For runs (Transfiguration / Perfect Transmutation) a single-component step removes only an END component. Returned components cannot be learned again until next turn
    ⚠ 1 counter regardless of how many components you return from that one spell. Same Enchantment capacity penalties as Reshape.
🌒

The Drought Has Begun

When It Happens

The instant the Source runs dry, the Drought begins.

Why the Opening matters: the component each player placed in the Arcane Reserve at set-up guarantees the Released Reserve holds enough for final preparations before the evaluation.
🔀

The Released Reserve

Immediately

  1. Shuffle the Array together with the Arcane Reserve
  2. This becomes the Released Reserve — a limited pool of unstable potential
  3. The Array no longer exists; turn order continues as normal
⚡

Drought Turns

1. Collection

Draw exactly 1 component from the Released Reserve — no choice; the Source and Array are gone

No Casting Phase

With the Source and Array gone, spells can't function — the phase simply doesn't exist during the Drought

2. Learning

Works completely normally — your last chance to optimize your Spellbook before evaluation

⚠️

Why Casting Stops

With ambient flow exhausted and all regulated channels collapsed, structured spellcasting can no longer be sustained:

❌ Conjurations

No bonus draws — they drew from the Source's organised reserves, now scattered and chaotic

❌ Transfigurations

No exchanges — they required the Array's stable framework, now dissolved

❌ Perfect Transmutations

Inert — they need both the Source and the Array; both are gone

✅ Enchantments

Still work — always active, never cast; they keep providing their full capacity benefits

No counters are placed on spells during the Drought — collect 1 component, then proceed directly to Learning.
🏁

The Contest Ends

When the Released Reserve is fully depleted, the contest ends immediately. The Academy evaluates whose Spellbook demonstrates the greatest breadth and depth of understanding.

How to Win

  1. Tally Recognition Points from all spells in your Spellbook
  2. The player with the most Recognition Points wins

Tiebreakers (in order)

  1. Most spells
  2. Most components in largest single spell

The Ascension Trials

Advanced Variant · For Experienced Players

An optional strategic finale that replaces the Standard Evaluation. The wizard who demonstrates the greatest mastery across six magical challenges becomes THE Archmage.

Strategy Primer: Building Toward the Trials

From the first turn, your Spellbook is also quietly shaping your options in the Ascension Trial. Each spell you learn can strengthen a particular kind of power: energy domains through Conjurations, transformation through Transfigurations, enchantment through Enchantments, and flexible pressure through Perfect Transmutations and some larger Enchantments.

A broad Spellbook gives you more ways to enter different trials and respond to what others might be building. A concentrated Spellbook can make one or two domains much harder to beat, but may leave you with fewer legal or efficient places to apply that strength.

Neither path is automatically better. As you build, keep asking: am I creating many possible trial entries, or am I making a smaller number of entries more commanding? The answer can change as the table reveals its ambitions.

Overview

The Process

  1. Calculate Power from each domain based on your spells
  2. Secretly allocate Power to 6 Trials
  3. Reveal simultaneously
  4. Highest legal power wins each Trial's Recognition Points
🔥

Power Calculation

3-4 components: Power = Length
5-6 components: Power = Length × 1.5 (round normally)
7+ components: Power = Length × 2.0

Examples

3 components= 3 power
5 components= 8 power (5 × 1.5 = 7.5 → 8)
7 components= 14 power
💫

Power Domains

Energy Power

Combined power from Conjurations in each energy type

Four separate domains (Radiance, Void, Flux, Aether)

Transformation Power

Total length of all Transfiguration spells

Perfect Transmutations count as Transfigurations

Enchantment Power

Total size of all Enchantments

Sum of all Enchantment component counts

✨

Wild Magic Power

Bonus allocation flexibility:

Sources

  • Each Perfect Transmutation: +1 wild magic
  • Each 4-component Enchantment: +1 wild magic

Rules

  • Only add to trials you're already attempting (with ≥1 normal power)
  • Doesn't count as a domain source
  • Affects total only (not tie-breakers)

Tie-Breakers

When trials tie, resolve in order:

  1. Utilization: Power used ÷ Total capacity of domains used
  2. Focus: Power on this trial ÷ Total power across all domains
  3. Breadth: More distinct sources (Harmony/Universal only)
  4. Shared Recognition: Both players get full points
