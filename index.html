A Wizards' Contest · 2–7 Players

Archmage · Ascension

Draw magical components from the depleting Source and arrange them into spells in your Spellbook. When the Drought ends, the wizard with the most refined Spellbook ascends.

Start here

New to the game? A competitive card game for 2–7 players, 30–60 minutes: build spells from four energies while the shared Source runs dry. The player site below uses the game's new title, Arcane Drought. Current rules: v3.1, under test.
