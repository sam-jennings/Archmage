Work done 2026-07-16 (later session — engine-builder question):
- EB analysis: the base game is a rummy/tableau hybrid with the EB spine (persistent
  spells, cast-driven card flow, capacity compounding) but lacking the four genre
  signatures — piece differentiation, chaining, a draft arc, escalation. Verdict:
  strengthen EB identity via a module, not the base loop (Session-4 depth brief;
  v3.1 mid confirm/kill; DR-HOOK protected). One deferred base-side option: deeper
  Conjuration draw ladder, gated on v3.1 landing.
- **Third v0.1 module developed:** `expansion/foci-and-conduits/` — Foci & Conduits
  (exclusive row of unique spell attachments = differentiation + draft; Tier-2
  Conduits = spell-to-spell chaining; never scores; deepens the Casting phase).
  Fit analysis, 12-bug iteration log, and open questions in house style.
- **Same-day revision (Sam's direction):** the free-claim draft was strictly
  clock-accelerating; Sam proposed a C&K-style secondary source. Adopted as the
  **Deep Source / Essence layer** — 5+ spells may convert cast-granted bonus draws
  into Essence (the spared card stays in the Source), and Essence is the only way
  to forge Foci (costs 2/3/4) / attach Conduits (2). Rejected variants logged:
  physical second deck (B10), Foci-in-the-deck (B11), free claiming (B12).

Work done 2026-07-17 (the 2p box):

  chosen: contested draft/market (cut-and-choose), per the deep dive's "design the 2p market

  cut-and-choose market replaces Collection: reveal 4, tithe 1 to the Arcane Reserve, sever 3
  into always-unequal shares, claim; Severance = choice seat + initiative in one token,
  contested every round; Last Schism: reveal 3, no tithe — a ~40%-of-rounds third act; Tier-2
  Rift-card deck ruled distinct from the dropped Arcane Weather). 14-bug iteration log, fit
  analysis, open questions in house style. First count-gated module in the line.
- **Same-day revisions from Sam's review:** B11 the Tithe (fixes Drought starvation — Reserve
  otherwise fed only by binding + scarce 2p TF discards), B12 open-information identity
  (veiled-core fix rejected; Veiled Power fuel tension noted), B13 Severance carries
  initiative (reverses the fixed-turn-order draft), B14 calibration flipped to reveal-4 after
  a scratch clock comparison (reveal-3/sever-2 gutted the cut for ~0.9 rounds of main game;
  Drought share fixed on the consumption side: ~41% vs base ~29%). Sim prereq before any live
  slot: 2p rig with cast-burn measured from real session logs — headline question is
  main-game length (~6.5 rounds, ~23% fewer casting phases than base).
