# 2026-07-06 — Protect the one-sentence hook

**Change:** Adopt an explicit protected hook as the product's core identity, and a
standing rule that judges future design changes by it. Accepted wording (Sam,
2026-07-06):

> **It's rummy where your melds stay alive as spell engines — but every spell you cast
> burns down the shared Source, and when it runs dry your Spellbook becomes the proof
> that decides who can renew the world's magic.**

**Standing rule:** no change may make this hook less visible at the table. Any redesign
of counters, scoring, Drought, or player count is judged first by whether the hook's
visible objects — the dying Source, the living Spellbook, the clock race, the earned
ending — become *more* visible, not by whether the numbers balance.

**Why:** The hook is the lens every other open decision is judged through (register
DR-HOOK; product viability §2/§6; design review §2). Making it explicit stops the
project optimizing subsystems that don't serve it.

**Amendment note:** the ending was changed from the register's recommended "the biggest
Spellbook wins" to "your Spellbook becomes the proof that decides who can renew the
world's magic." This is deliberate: it reframes the win condition as its *thematic
stakes* (the strongest Spellbook's owner ascends and renews the depleting magic) rather
than a bare scoring statement — directly answering the product-viability finding that
the theme is currently invisible at the table. It should stay consistent with the lore
in `rulebook/Story.md`; the mechanical win condition (highest Recognition Points) is
unchanged.

**Canon delta:** none — the hook is a lens/identity statement, not a rule. Recorded in
PROJECT.md, not `canon.yml`.
