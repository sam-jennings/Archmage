# Expansion status reset: gate lifted, no settled sequencing, only Trials confirmed

## Change

1. **The expansion design gate is lifted.** Paper design work on expansions may proceed at any time; it no longer waits on the core-loop lock. (Playtest *slots* remain a per-session priority call — expansion tests still compete with base-game P1s like the v3.1 confirm/kill session.)
2. **Ascension Trials is the only locked-in expansion.** Everything else in `expansion/` is a collection of ideas at different stages of development — nothing tested, nothing confirmed.
3. **There is no settled expansion sequencing.** The "Schools + Last Rites first" development choice (Post-Echo Exploration, Part 3) is downgraded from a commitment to a recommendation-on-paper. Docs must not present any sequencing as decided.

## Why

Sam, 2026-07-16, while commissioning development of two new expansion concepts (Rituals & Mastery Seals; hidden traitor): "Ignore expansion gating. The only expansion I have locked in is Ascension Trials … There is no settled sequencing for Schools + Last Rites first, just a collection of expansion ideas at different stages of development."

## Canon delta
