# 2026-07-06 — D-AT1: secret allocation stays

Source: Sam, in the hybrid-rebuild critique session (2026-07-06), resolving D-AT1 from
`_review/ASCENSION_TRIALS_01_ENTRY_CAP_REBUILD_SUPERSEDED_2026-07-06.md` §7.

## Decision

The Ascension Trials' core mechanic is **secret allocation of limited domain power across
Trials** (calculate → secretly allocate → reveal → highest legal allocation wins). The
entry-cap commitment model from the ground-up rebuild is **rejected** as a replacement.
Sam's constraint: do not replace secret allocation unless an overwhelming case exists;
review found none — the mechanic is game-theoretically sound (asymmetric-budget Blotto).

This decision fixes the *mechanic identity* only. The hybrid ruleset built on it
(`_review/ASCENSION_TRIALS_02_HYBRID_REBUILD_PROPOSAL_2026-07-06.md`) and the critique's proposed fixes
(`_review/ASCENSION_TRIALS_03_HYBRID_REBUILD_CRITIQUE_2026-07-06.md`) remain proposals gated on
playtests (D-AT2, D-AT3 still open).

## Canon delta

None — AT is a variant; no vocabulary/structure change to `meta/canon.yml`.
