# Solo direction — base-box automa, The Attuned Rival

## Change

Solo play is a **base-box feature**, not an expansion: a rules-driven automa (the
Attuned Rival) targeting a "1–4 players" box line. The deep solo/co-op experience
(demand deck, system-as-opponent, co-op face) stays entirely with the co-op expansion
direction (audit #9) — no split of that concept across products.

Chassis chosen from three candidates (Hunter / Attuned Rival / Severance, each × rules
brain or deck brain): **Attuned Rival on the rules brain** — the Rival owns one energy
suit as its own stack; Flip/Tithe/Burn turn; row scored on the real table. Full v0.1
ruleset: `expansion/solo-attuned-rival/Solo Mode - The Attuned Rival (v0.1).md`.
The Severance chassis is parked as the Schism's potential 1–2p extension; the deck
brain is the production form if the chassis confirms.

## Why

- Publishers read solo support as a base-game expectation (Wingspan precedent: Automa
  in the box); the expansion audit lists solo/co-op as "owned but empty — the largest
  unserved audience".
- Sam's criterion from the exploration: the automa's state must change the player's
  decisions each turn; the Attuned Rival scored best on that test at zero new
  components (one reference card).
- A base-box automa preserves the competitive race (same teach, doubles as practice
  mode); the co-op direction keeps the "different experience" axis.

## Scope and gates

- The v0.1 ruleset is a **draft, untested**. This decision settles the *direction and
  product placement*, not the rules. Numeric tuning is gated on the v3.1 confirm/kill
  outcome (score-table dependency), then the module's own sim + confirm/kill plan
  (doc §14).
- Solo is 1p count-gated — the Echo compatibility rule is satisfied vacuously (second
  count-gated module after The Schism).
