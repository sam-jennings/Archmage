# 2026-07-06 — F4 Enchantment scoring 12/18 override rationale

Source: `_review/ARCHMAGE_SAM_DECISIONS_F3_F14_2026-07.md`, §F4.

## Decision

Keep Enchantment scoring at **6 / 12 / 18** (3 / 4 / 5-component). Do **not** reopen the
15/26 recommendation from `simulation/SCORING_REBALANCE.md`. Record the override
rationale and treat the value as a **live-play watch item**, not a settled balance proof.

## Why

Enchantment's primary reward is **capacity**. The 5-component Enchantment already grants
Unlimited Capacity — the strongest capacity reward in the game — so its Recognition
Point value should not *also* price the full rarity of that advantage. A 26-point value
double-pays the dominant mechanical edge and re-creates the rich-get-richer Drought the
ladder was designed to fix.

## Playtest watch (carry into 5–6p Echo + AT tests)

- Does a 5-card Enchantment feel under-rewarded at 18?
- Does Unlimited Capacity already feel like enough without a higher score?
- Does the UC holder become the Drought favourite too reliably?
- Do players pursue the 5-card Enchantment for meaning, or ignore it as too cheap at 18?

## Canon delta

None — 6/12/18 is already canon (`meta/canon.yml` `enchantment_ladder`). This decision
records *why* the SCORING_REBALANCE verdict was overridden.
