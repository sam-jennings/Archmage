## Recommendation (Q2)

At 2p, with a **free** end-game reshape the best consolidation adds **+27.7** points over the as-played book and lands on a Transfiguration **47%** of the time (all-in-one `unbounded` = +23.8). Charging **one counter per spell broken** brings the marginal to **+21.0** (→TF 32%): players hold **7.0** counters and **6.0** committed spells on average, and can afford to break the whole book in only **72%** of games.

Part A shows **no** TF-vs-PT pricing inversion under this table (the easier spell no longer scores more) — see the pricing rows above.

Compare against the canon baseline in `SCORING_EV.md`. Candidate analysis only — nothing here is canon.

## Model limitations

- Greedy, identical, non-opponent-aware agents; no contest for the Array. A 2p exploit may be partly regulated by blocking at higher counts (Session 4 R2) — Part B measures the pool, not live contention.

- Hand-model reach-probabilities are chase ceilings, not in-play attainment.

- The Reshape evaluator forges the single best spell from the pool (vs keeping the committed book); multi-spell pool partitioning is a documented v2 refinement.

- Conjuration card values are captured exactly (rig `vals` field), so the pool reconstruction is exact for hand + enchantment + conjuration cards.
